Google announced the Gemini Decisions API, a multimodal decision endpoint tuned for speed and calibrated confidence, and DeepMind shipped AlphaGenome 2 for reading how cells interpret DNA. On the security side: eMudhra revoked eight more intermediate CAs it had earlier declared unaffected, November's Patch Tuesday fixes roughly 1,100 Microsoft vulnerabilities including one already exploited, and researchers found tens of thousands of Grok-built apps publicly exposed, some leaking credentials.
