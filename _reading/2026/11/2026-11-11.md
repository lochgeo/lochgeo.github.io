A bigger day from the labs than yesterday: Anthropic shipped its most capable models for daunting technical work, Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 (Mythos, the bug-finding model, is already headed into Cisco and IBM products); Google DeepMind used its new AlphaProof Code to formally verify Signal's protocol; and Meta introduced Muse Ember, a small model built for the cheap, repetitive work agents do all day. Meta Engineering also published a deep dive on TLX, its hardware-native GPU compiler for production ML. Beyond AI: Git patched a flaw that lets malicious repositories execute code during recursive clones, an SGLang RCE was used to breach NEAR AI and steal data from 18 agents, and Fireworks AI confirmed a wider breach touching customer datasets, model weights and Notion AI chats.
