Today's list leans toward agents and the machinery underneath them. Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights model small enough to run on a laptop, and OpenAI published both a technical account of how it defends against adversarial distillation and a new benchmark for medicinal-chemistry reasoning. Amazon's threat intelligence team describes AI agents that found and exploited a zero-day at a university without human direction, Signal explains why it now blocks the AI assistants built into iOS and Android from reading its screens, and Christian Ekrem argues that reviewing code is not a substitute for writing it. Beyond AI: a KVM bug that let a guest VM read 127 GB of a cloud host's memory, PHP 8.6 tightening its defaults, and SonicWall telling Gen 6 firewall owners to replace the hardware rather than wait for patches.
