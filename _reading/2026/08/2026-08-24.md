Last week I watched a teammate vibe-code a feature that would've taken me two days, and the agent produced something that passed tests but smelled wrong — defensive error handling where we needed invariants, duplicated logic where we needed a shared abstraction. That's the tension right now: agents are fast, but they don't feel the maintenance pain. The pieces that caught my eye this week are the ones grappling with that gap — GitHub's canvas model for making agent work visible and steerable, Armin Ronacher pulling back the curtain on how reasoning traces actually work, and Linus Torvalds admitting the AI helped but also tried to quit on him. Worth your time.
